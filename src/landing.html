The best part of book club was always the talking.

So Bookish gives the chat a few new ways to play. Nobody writes a recap, nobody keeps score, and nobody has to finish the book to have a good night.

Say it without your name on it.

The Tea is your club's anonymous corner. Drop a take about the book, or quietly admit you didn't finish. It's about the story, never each other.

Your living room, your rules.

Most clubs meet in someone's home. Pick how findable yours is, and change it whenever you like.

Open

On the map in your city. Anyone nearby can join.

Private

On the map. People ask, and a host says yes.

Hidden

Off the map. The only way in is an invite code.
The map only ever shows your city. Never a street, never a living room. Members see where you meet once they're in.
